Basis of Admission Exclusions

Students with any selected Admission Basis code are excluded from grade-level enrollment counts. The excluded total appears in the "BOA Excluded" column on the Elementary Registration report.

Special Exclusion Field

Enter a table (Students or a one-to-one Students extension table) and field. Students where this field is not null/empty are excluded from grade-level counts and totaled in the "Special" column on the Elementary Registration report.
